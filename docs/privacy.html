Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

This policy applies to the Android app Tuition Khata (package com.tuitionkhata.app), published by Arpan Halder ("we", "us").

1. Summary

Tuition Khata works completely offline. We do not collect, sell or share your data, and we run no servers. Everything you enter stays on your own phone, unless you choose to turn on optional Google Drive backup (see section 3).

2. Information you enter

To keep your tuition records you may enter student names, guardian names, phone numbers, fee amounts, payment records and notes, plus your own name (optional). This information is saved only in a SQLite database inside the app's private storage on your device. We have no servers and cannot see, access or recover it.

3. Permissions and internet

The app requests the internet permission only for the optional Google Drive backup. If you connect your Google account, the app saves one backup file of your records in the private, hidden app-data area of your own Google Drive (scope drive.appdata). Only this app can access that file; we cannot see it, and it never passes through any server of ours. Your Google email is shown in the app and stored on your phone only to remember which account is connected. You can disconnect at any time in Settings and delete the backup from your Google Account settings (Drive > Manage apps). The app has no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking libraries, and does not use your location, camera, microphone or contacts.

Tuition Khata's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Reminders (WhatsApp / SMS / Call)

When you tap a reminder button, the app opens WhatsApp, your messaging app or your phone dialer with a pre-filled message. The message is then handled by that app under its own privacy policy. We do not receive it.

5. Backup and export

The "Export data" feature uses Android's share sheet so you can save a copy of your records wherever you choose (for example, to yourself on a messaging app or a notes app). Where it goes is your decision; we do not receive it. The app's own data is excluded from Android cloud auto-backup.

6. Data deletion

You can delete any student, batch or payment inside the app. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, permanently deletes all data. Because we hold no copy, deletion is entirely in your control.

7. Children

The app is a tool for tutors (adults). Records may concern minors who are your students; you are responsible for handling that information appropriately. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

8. Security

Data lives in the app's private sandbox, which other apps cannot read on a non-rooted device. Please protect your phone with a screen lock. If you share an export, treat it as confidential.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new date.

10. Contact

Questions? Open an issue on GitHub Issues.
