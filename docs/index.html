Made for private tutors

If you teach batches or students at home or in a coaching class, Tuition Khata replaces the paper register. Add students, set monthly fees, record payments, and see at a glance who still owes money.

₹ Indian Rupee first

Amounts use Indian grouping (₹12,34,567). Switch to USD, EUR, GBP, AED, BDT and more — per student or batch.

Month-wise dues

Automatic month-by-month ledger. Part payments and advance payments are handled correctly.

Batches

Group students into batches with a default fee, so adding a new student takes seconds.

Reminders

Send a polite fee reminder over WhatsApp or SMS in one tap — in Bengali or English.

100% offline

Data is stored in a SQLite database on your phone. No account, no internet, no ads, no tracking.

বাংলা & English

The whole app works in Bengali and English. Switch any time in Settings.
